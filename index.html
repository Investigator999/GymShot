    Sharing to GymShot

    Android (Chrome)

    1. Open this page in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Install app (or "Add to Home screen").
    2. In Instagram / TikTok / YouTube tap Share on a reel.
    3. Pick GymShot in the share sheet (it may be under “More”). Done — tag it and save.

    iPhone (Safari)

    1. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
    2. iOS doesn't let web apps appear in the share sheet, so in Instagram tap Share → Copy link.
    3. Open GymShot and tap Paste — then tag it and save.

    Your saves live only on this device. Use Export backup now and then.
